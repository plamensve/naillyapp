Account & data

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Nailly account from the app. If you cannot access the app, you can also send an external deletion request using the instructions below.

Delete from the Nailly app

  1. Open Nailly and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile and tap Settings.
  3. Under Privacy & Data, select Delete account & data.
  4. Review the permanent-action warning and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without app access

If you cannot access Nailly, email svetoslavov.plamen@gmail.com with the subject Nailly account deletion request. Send the request from the email address associated with your Nailly account where possible. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request to protect your account from unauthorised deletion.

Email an account deletion request →

What is deleted

The deletion flow is designed to remove the Nailly authentication account and associated profile data, owned studio information, portfolio records and images, saved looks and other account-associated records. Appointment-linked or other limited information may be retained only where necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or dispute handling.

Before you delete

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want to change your password, profile photo or other settings, open Profile → Settings instead.

Processing time for external requests

We will process verified deletion requests without undue delay and within the time limits required by applicable data-protection law. In-app deletion is generally the fastest option when you can still access your account.
