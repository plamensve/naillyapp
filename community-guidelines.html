Community · Safety

Community Guidelines

Effective 1 October 2026. Nailly is built for respectful visual inspiration, genuine studio discovery and real appointment experiences.

Be authentic

Use accurate account and studio information. Do not impersonate another person, artist, studio or business, and do not misrepresent your identity, portfolio or services.

Publish appropriate content

Upload nail-related content you own or have permission to use. Do not publish illegal, hateful, sexually explicit, exploitative, violent, harassing, privacy-invasive, deceptive or clearly unrelated content.

Respect intellectual property

Do not upload images or other content in a way that infringes copyright, trademarks or other rights. If you believe your work has been used without permission, report the content in Nailly or contact support.

Use bookings honestly

Appointment tools are for genuine requests. Repeated fake bookings, deliberate abuse of availability, fraudulent activity or patterns of malicious no-shows may lead to restrictions.

Keep reviews genuine

Reviews should relate to real appointment experiences and remain relevant. Do not fabricate reviews, trade ratings, threaten users, publish discriminatory content or use reviews to extort, harass or retaliate.

Reporting and blocking

You can report designs or reviews and block studios from within Nailly. Reports are sent to Nailly's moderation workflow. Blocking changes what you see and does not notify the blocked studio.

Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, Nailly may dismiss a report, remove or unpublish content, restrict features, suspend an account or take other action needed to protect users, comply with law or enforce these rules.

Contact

For safety, copyright or community concerns, use the in-app reporting tools where available or contact Nailly Support.
