Legal · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026. This policy explains how Nailly processes personal data when you use the Nailly mobile application, website and related services.

Who is responsible for your data

Nailly is operated by Plamen Svetoslavov Krastev, Bulgaria. For privacy questions or requests, contact svetoslavov.plamen@gmail.com or +359 883 427273.

Data we process

Depending on how you use Nailly, we may process your name, email address, account role, profile photo, city, bio, studio name, address, phone number, studio description, portfolio images, saved designs, availability, appointment requests and statuses, ratings, reviews, reports, blocked-account relationships and technical information needed to authenticate, secure and operate the service.

Photos, AI analysis and visual search

Portfolio images are stored so they can be displayed in Nailly. When a studio uploads a design, the image may be analysed to identify manicure characteristics and support visual discovery. When you choose an inspiration image for visual search, that image may be transmitted to Nailly's matching infrastructure to compare it with published portfolio designs. These images are not intended to be used for unrelated advertising or profiling.

Why we process data

We process data to create and secure accounts, provide profiles and studio pages, publish portfolios, save favourites, manage appointment requests, provide ratings and reviews, operate visual search, moderate content, investigate reports, prevent abuse, communicate service messages, troubleshoot problems and comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, processing is based as appropriate on performance of the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing Nailly, your consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Service providers

Nailly uses third-party infrastructure to provide the service. This currently includes Supabase for authentication, database and file storage; Resend for transactional email delivery; and cloud infrastructure used for Nailly's visual-search and AI matching services. Apple and Google may process data when you download or use Nailly through their app stores. Providers process data under their own terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

International data transfers

Some providers may process data outside your country or the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions or contractual protections are used by the relevant provider.

Retention

Account data is generally retained while your account is active. Some information may be kept for a limited period where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance. Account deletion is designed to remove the account and associated user-generated data unless retention is legally required.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of certain data and lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. You can update some information directly in Nailly and delete your account from Profile → Settings → Privacy & Data.

Security

We use authentication, access controls, row-level security, moderation controls and other technical and organisational measures designed to protect Nailly data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Nailly is not designed for children under 16. If we learn that personal data was provided by a child in circumstances where parental authorisation is required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or restrict that data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Nailly evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
