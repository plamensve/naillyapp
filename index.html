01 / The experience

From screenshot to salon chair

Nail inspiration is everywhere.
Booking it should be effortless.

Nailly is a nail art inspiration and salon booking app built around visual discovery. Browse manicure ideas, use photo-based matching to find similar nail designs, compare artist portfolios and move from inspiration to an appointment without searching across feeds and DMs.

See it.
Save it.

Browse nail art ideas and manicure designs, save favourites, or search with a photo to discover visually similar looks. Open a design to explore the nail studio, artist portfolio, pricing and availability behind it.
